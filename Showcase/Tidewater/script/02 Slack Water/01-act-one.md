# Act One

= Ten years on, the clock is gone, the light is automatic, and Ellis has a daughter who asks too many questions.

EXT. SALTMARROW HARBOUR - DAWN {#s012 color=blue}

The same grey water. The same gulls. The lighthouse turns, but nobody climbs it now: a white box on the gallery rail blinks its own green light.

ELLIS HALE (30s), a fisherman's sweater with the elbows gone, walks the jetty with MAY (9), who carries an empty crab pot like a lantern.

MAY
Aunt Juno's pots were always empty too. You said.

ELLIS
(stopping)
I never told you that.

INT. THE LIGHTHOUSE - KEEPER'S ROOM - DAY {#s013}

Dust sheets. The kettle is still here. So is the floorboard, nailed down tight, and a child's chalk drawing of a clock on it.

May kneels by the board. Ellis stands in the doorway and does not come in.

ELLIS
Who drew that?

MAY
It was here.

EXT. THE DROWNED MARKET - NIGHT {#s014 color=red tags=mystery}

Low tide, lower than the charts allow. [[The Drowned Market]] is back. Stalls rise out of the mud with their awnings still up.

At the far end, a lamp is lit. Someone is keeping the light.

ELLIS
(barely)
Juno?
