# Act One

= Juno keeps the light her brother died under, and finds the clock that could undo it.

EXT. SALTMARROW HARBOUR - DAWN {#s001 color=blue}

Grey water. Gulls argue over a net. The town of Saltmarrow stacks itself up the cliff like crates nobody unloaded.

Above it all, THE LIGHTHOUSE turns its slow white eye.

JUNO HALE (34), oilskin coat, salt in her hair, hauls a crab pot onto the jetty. It is empty. It is always empty.

JUNO (V.O.)
Every town has a night it doesn't talk about. Ours has a tide.

INT. THE LIGHTHOUSE - KEEPER'S ROOM - MORNING {#s002}

Brass, charts, and a kettle that has outlived three keepers. OLD TOBIAS WREN (70s), all knuckles and patience, polishes a lens the size of a cartwheel.

Juno drops the crab pot by the door.

TOBIAS
Nothing again?

JUNO
The sea's keeping it all for itself.

TOBIAS
(not looking up)
The sea keeps what it's owed. Your brother knew that.

Juno stops. A beat too long.

JUNO
Don't.

TOBIAS
Thirty-seven years, girl. You can say his name in this room.

She doesn't.

INT. THE LIGHTHOUSE - LAMP ROOM - NIGHT {#s003}

The great lens turns. Light sweeps over Juno's face, then away, then back.

She pries up a loose floorboard. Beneath it: a wooden box, swollen with damp. Carved on the lid: a wave, and a clock face where the moon should be.

Inside, wrapped in sailcloth, is [[The Tidewater Clock]]. Its single hand points backwards.

JUNO
(whispering)
Ellis. What did you find?

She winds it. Once.

The lighthouse beam stops mid-sweep.

Outside the window, the tide pulls *out* when it should be coming *in*.

EXT. SALTMARROW HARBOUR - NIGHT {#s004 dur=1:15 color=red}

1987. Rain like thrown gravel. Boats strain at their ropes. The harbour lamps are gas, not electric.

Juno stands on the jetty, soaked, the clock clutched to her chest.

A BOY (12) runs past her, laughing, a lantern swinging. ELLIS HALE. Her brother. Alive.

JUNO
_Ellis!_

He doesn't hear. He's gone into the dark toward [[The Drowned Market]].

INT. HARBOUR TICKET HUT - NIGHT {#s011}

A shed the size of a wardrobe. A paraffin heater ticks. Timetables pinned three deep, the newest already curling.

The HARBOUR MASTER (60s), moustache like a yard brush, counts coins into a cash tin and doesn't look up.

Juno slaps a note on the counter. Bright plastic. A hologram winks in the lamplight.

HARBOUR MASTER
What's that, then?

JUNO
Money. For the late boat. The market boat.

He holds it up to the lamp. Turns it over. The hologram winks at him too.

HARBOUR MASTER
(laughing)
Queen's looking well for her age.

JUNO
Please. My brother's out there.

HARBOUR MASTER
Everybody's brother's out there, love. No boats tonight. Not in this.

The heater ticks. Rain finds a gap in the roof and taps the counter between them.

Juno takes the note back. Folds it small. Puts it in her pocket beside the clock.

JUNO
Then I'll walk.

She's gone before he can tell her there's no path. He looks at the empty doorway a long moment, then at the coins in the tin, as if one of them might be lying too.

INT. HARBOUR OFFICE - CONTINUOUS {#s005 color=red tags=1987}

A cramped room of ledgers. CAPTAIN IDA MARSH (40s), Greyline Salvage badge on her coat, signs a manifest without reading it.

IDA
Tell the boy to stay off the market tonight.

HARBOUR MASTER (O.S.)
He never listens, Captain.

IDA
Then he'll learn the way the rest of us did. The hard way.

She blots the ink. Through the window, a figure in an oilskin coat watches her. Juno.

Ida looks up. Their eyes meet.

IDA (CONT'D)
(to herself)
That coat's not from round here.

FADE OUT.
