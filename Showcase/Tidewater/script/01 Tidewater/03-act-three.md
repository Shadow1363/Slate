# Act Three

= Two turns back, Juno gets there first. The price is her place in the story.

EXT. THE DROWNED MARKET - EARLIER THAT NIGHT {#s009 color=red tags=1987}

The market before the storm: crowded, bright, loud. Ellis is buying sweets.

Juno, older than this night has any right to see, kneels in front of him.

JUNO
Ellis. Look at me. What's the rule about the market?

ELLIS
(suspicious)
Never after the bell.

JUNO
Good boy. The bell goes in ten minutes.

He looks at her a long time.

ELLIS
Do I know you?

JUNO
(the hardest line she has ever said)
No. But you will. Go home.

He goes.

Across the market, Captain Ida Marsh watches, a [[Greyline Salvage]] crew at her back. She marks something in a notebook.

INT. THE LIGHTHOUSE - KEEPER'S ROOM - DAY {#s010 color=green}

2024. The same kettle. Tobias, old again, pours two cups.

An ELLIS HALE (49), alive, grey at the temples, sits across from him.

ELLIS
Strange thing. I dreamed about a woman in an oilskin coat.

TOBIAS
Did you now.

ELLIS
Felt like I owed her something.

Tobias glances at the floorboard. It's nailed down tight.

TOBIAS
(after a moment)
The sea keeps what it's owed.

On the mantel: a photograph of the Hale family. Mother, father, a boy. No daughter.

>THE END<
