# Tidewater

A lighthouse keeper's daughter finds a clock that winds the tide back one night at a time, and spends herself undoing her brother's drowning.

## The hook
[[Juno Hale]] keeps [[The Lighthouse]] where she grew up. Her brother [[Ellis Hale]] drowned in [[The Great Storm]] of 1987.

## Act one: the clock
She finds [[The Tidewater Clock]] under the lamp room floor and winds it once.

## Act two: the rules
Back in 1987 she meets young [[Tobias Wren|Tobias]] and learns the [[Tide Rules]] the hard way.

### The clock
One turn, one night back. It only works at the slack of the tide.

### The price of a turn
Every turn takes something small from the present. She doesn't notice at first.

## Act three: the price
She saves Ellis, and the sea takes her place in the family. [[Greyline Salvage]] was watching all along.
