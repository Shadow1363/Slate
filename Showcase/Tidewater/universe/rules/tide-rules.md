## How it works
Each turn of [[The Tidewater Clock]] sends the winder back one night, to the same tide.

## Limits and costs
- The winder arrives where the clock was that night.
- The sea takes something the winder didn't think to guard (the **keeper's due**).
- Wind only at slackwater, or the beam stops.

## Where it breaks
Winding twice in one night skips a night entirely. Juno does this in act two.
