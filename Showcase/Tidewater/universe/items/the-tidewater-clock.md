## What it is
A brass clock in a carved box: a wave, and a clock face where the moon should be. One hand, pointing backwards.

## Where it has been
Found by [[Ellis Hale]] at [[The Drowned Market]] in 1987, buried by [[Tobias Wren]], found again by [[Juno Hale]]. See [[Tide Rules]].
